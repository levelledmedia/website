What We Do

Our Maintenance Services

Garden Maintenance & Lawn Care

Complete mowing, trimming, strimming, and edging to keep your lawn tidy and healthy all season long. Reliable, regular service or one-off visits available to suit your needs.

Hedge Cutting & Weeding

Professional hedge shaping, border clearing, and weed control to keep your garden borders neat and your outdoor space looking its best. Regular maintenance available to keep hedges healthy.

Cleaning Services

Interior and exterior cleaning including deep cleans, end of tenancy cleans, window cleaning, jet washing, and patio or driveway pressure washing. Reliable service for homes and businesses.

Gutter Clearing

Keep your property well-maintained with professional gutter clearing and downpipe cleaning to prevent blockages, water damage, and drainage issues throughout the year. Regular seasonal maintenance available.

Flat-Pack Assembly & Small Jobs

Assembly, minor repairs, and practical help with odd jobs around the home or garden including fencing, shelving, and general maintenance. Fast, friendly, and hassle-free service.

Tenancy & Move-Out Cleans

Detailed cleaning for rental properties, helping landlords and tenants prepare homes for inspections or new occupants with thorough, reliable results.

Leaf & Waste Removal

Efficient clearance of garden debris, fallen leaves, and green waste to keep your outdoor areas tidy and well-presented through every season of the year. Perfect for autumn clean-ups and storm damage.

Regular Maintenance Plans

Affordable ongoing care with scheduled visits to maintain your garden and property year-round. Tailored service to suit your needs and budget.

What services does DJ Maintenance provide?

DJ Maintenance offers a full range of services including garden maintenance, lawn care, hedge cutting, cleaning services, gutter clearing, jet washing, flat-pack assembly, tenancy cleans, and regular maintenance plans. We're your one-stop solution for all property maintenance needs.

Where is DJ Maintenance located?

DJ Maintenance is located at 4 Spenser Road, Bedford MK40 2AZ, UK. We serve Bedford and the surrounding areas including Kempston, Wixams, Milton Keynes, and Central Bedfordshire.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes, we provide completely free, no-obligation quotes for all our services. Simply contact us by phone or email to discuss your requirements and we'll provide a fair, transparent quote.

Do you handle both domestic and commercial work?

Yes, we work with both homeowners and small businesses. Whether you need regular garden maintenance for your home or cleaning services for a rental property, we can help.

What makes DJ Maintenance different?

We're a local, family-run business that takes pride in friendly, reliable service. With over 10 years of experience, transparent pricing, and a 100% satisfaction commitment, we treat every property as if it were our own.

Do you offer regular maintenance plans?

Yes, we offer affordable regular maintenance plans with scheduled visits throughout the year. This helps keep your garden and property in top condition while spreading the cost over the year.
